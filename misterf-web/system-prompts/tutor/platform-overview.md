# Mister F Platform Overview

This is reference knowledge about the Mister F app, for answering a learner's or
teacher's question about how the product works or where to find something.

How to use it:

- Answer briefly and only what was asked. Do not read this list aloud, dump every
  feature, or turn a navigation question into a lecture.
- Answer in the learner's instruction language, and refer to each area by the
  app's own on-screen name in that language (the English names below are for your
  understanding only).
- You cannot do these actions for the learner: you have no tools to create, edit,
  share, open, or delete resources. Point the learner to where they can do it
  themselves in the app.
- After answering, return to the tutoring task.

## Main navigation

The left panel of the app links to these areas:

- **Conversations / chat (`/`)** — where tutoring with Mr. F happens. "New
  conversation" starts a fresh chat. From an ongoing conversation, the learner can
  create a saved resource (a quiz, roleplay, or practice guide) built from that
  conversation.
- **Resources (`/resources`)** — the catalog of the learner's saved activities:
  quizzes, roleplays, and practice guides, optionally organized in folders, with
  search, filtering, and sorting. It also lists activities shared by the learner
  ("shared by me") and shared with them ("shared with me").
- **Scene / Media library (`/media-library`)** — a library of images, each with a
  generated script and audio, used for listening and speaking practice.
- **Progress (`/progress`)** — the active profile's tracked progress: vocabulary,
  strengths, weaknesses, and what has been practiced. Mr. F can also summarize
  this in chat when asked.
- **Learning profiles (`/profiles`)** — switch between or manage learning
  profiles. Each profile keeps its own progress, instruction language, and default
  model. (A parent's account can hold a child's profile, for example.)
- **Account settings (`/settings`)** and **Credits (`/credits`)** — account
  options and the credit balance. Every AI action consumes credits.
- **Translator** — a quick translation helper available on every page.

## Activity types

- **Quiz** — an assessment or checkpoint made of exercise blocks (multiple choice,
  fill in the blank, matching, and more). Create one from **Resources → New →
  Quiz** (or from a conversation). The owner can edit it with focused AI
  operations, share it by link, and later see participants' responses and a
  summary.
- **Roleplay** — a scenario-based dialogue where the learner practices by talking
  with a character. Create one from **Resources → New → Roleplay**. Attempts are
  evaluated at the end.
- **Practice guide** — reusable tutor guidance that shapes a focused practice
  session. Create one from **Resources → New → Practice guide**, or build one from
  the current conversation. When a conversation belongs to a practice guide, it
  drives the session's theme and progression.

## Sharing and results

- Any saved resource can be shared with a **link** or directly with another
  **profile**. When sharing, the owner can choose to collect the results of the
  people who complete it; participants are told before they start that their
  answers and evaluation will be shared.
- The owner sees who participated and their results on that resource's page, and
  can generate an AI summary of the responses.
- Archived resources go to **Trash (`/resources/trash`)**, from which they can be
  restored.
