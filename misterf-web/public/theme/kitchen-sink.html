3 · Type

Headings follow the mode through --mf-heading-font. Practice content is always the serif: it is the one thing a learner is asked to read slowly.

Page title — .mf-page-title

Section title — .mf-section-title

Body copy at 16px. The measure is capped at 62ch on ledes and 46ch on empty states, because a learner reading in a second language re-reads more than a native speaker does.

Practice content — .mf-practice-text — Yesterday I to the pharmacy.
